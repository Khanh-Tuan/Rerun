AFTERLAP

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Afterlap is an arcade game for iPhone and the web, made by Tuan Khanh Dam. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • You can play without an account, and the game works offline.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We don't sell or share your information, and we don't use it for advertising.
  • If you create an account, we keep your email address only to send you sign-in codes. It is never shown to anyone.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • You can delete your account and everything on our server from inside the game.

1. Information stored only on your device

Your progress is saved on your own device so it's there next time you play: best scores, stars, skins, awards, daily challenges, statistics, settings (such as sound) and the name you use for online play. This stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app (or, in a web browser, when you clear the site's data).

2. Information we collect when you play

Leaderboard runs (all players, with or without an account)

To keep the leaderboards fair, the game asks our server to time each solo run. When you start the game for the first time, it creates a random player ID for your device. It isn't linked to your name, email, phone or Apple ID.

For each run, the server stores:

  • your random player ID;
  • when the run started and finished, your score (laps) and close calls;
  • the times at which you switched lanes during the run. These are used only to check that scores are genuine.

If you don't have an account, none of this is shown to anyone. Runs we no longer need are deleted automatically after about two days. Your single best run is kept, so you can claim it on the leaderboards if you create an account later.

If the game can't reach our server (for example, you're offline), you can still play. That run just won't count for the leaderboards.

Your country

To suggest a flag for the leaderboards, the game uses your device's time zone and the country of your internet connection. The country is worked out by our hosting provider's network, and we keep only the two-letter country code, not your IP address. You can confirm or change the country, or hide it completely. If you hide it, you appear only on the Global leaderboard, without a flag.

If you create an account (optional)

  • Email address. Used only to send you one-time sign-in codes. It is never shown to other players, and we never use it for marketing or send you newsletters.
  • Display name. You choose it. It is shown publicly on the leaderboards.
  • Country (unless you hide it). Shown as a flag next to your name.
  • Your best score, rank and chosen skin. Shown on the leaderboards.
  • Whether you're playing right now. Other players see a small "online" dot next to your name while you're playing. We store the time you were last active for this.
  • Your online save. A copy of your game progress (best scores, stars, skins you own, awards, daily challenges and play statistics), so you can continue on another device or after reinstalling.

Playing online with a friend

When you play in an online room, the game sends your chosen name, the room code, your lap count, close calls and whether you're still playing, so your friend can see them. This is visible only to the other player in your room. Rooms are deleted automatically within a day of closing.

3. Purchases

Premium skins are optional, cosmetic-only purchases made through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment. We never receive your card details, billing address or Apple ID password. The game only learns which skins your Apple ID owns, so it can unlock them. If you have an account, the skins you own are part of your online save. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Premium skins can't be bought in the web version.

4. How we use information

We use the information above only to run the game:

  • timing runs and checking that scores are genuine;
  • showing leaderboards and ranks;
  • signing you in and saving your progress online;
  • running online matches;
  • unlocking skins you have bought.

We don't sell, rent or share your information, we don't use it for advertising or profiling, and we don't combine it with information from other sources.

5. Who processes it for us

  • Supabase (supabase.com) runs our game server and database on our behalf. Like any hosting provider, its network may briefly record technical information such as IP addresses in security and error logs, which we don't use to identify you.
  • Our email provider sends sign-in codes on our behalf. It receives only your email address and the code.
  • Apple handles App Store purchases.

These providers may store data on servers outside your country. They are only allowed to use it to provide their service to us.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Play without an account. Nothing about you is shown publicly.
  • Hide your country in your profile at any time.
  • Change your display name in your profile.
  • Delete your account in the game: Ranks > your name > Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, email address, display name, leaderboard entries, runs and online save from our server. Progress on your device stays until you delete the app.
  • Access, correct or delete your data, or get a copy of it. Depending on where you live (for example, in the UK, the EU or California), you have legal rights to do this. Contact us at the address below. If you play without an account, tell us roughly when you played and we'll do our best to find your data. We'll reply within 30 days.

If you're in the UK or the EU, we rely on these legal bases: providing the game you asked for (contract), and keeping the leaderboards fair and the game secure (legitimate interests). You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Children

Afterlap is suitable for all ages, and accounts are optional. Public information is limited to a chosen display name, an optional country flag, a skin and game scores. The game is not directed at children under 13 for account creation. If you are a parent and believe your child has given us their email address, contact us and we will delete the account.

9. Security

Data is sent to our server over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to the database is restricted so that players can only read public leaderboard information and change their own data. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep what we collect to a minimum.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version at this address and update the date at the top. If the changes are significant, we'll also mention them in the game.

11. Contact
