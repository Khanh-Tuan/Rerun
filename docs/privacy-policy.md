# Privacy Policy for Afterlap

_Last updated: [DATE]_

Afterlap is an arcade game made by [YOUR NAME OR STUDIO].

## What we collect

When you play on your own, nothing. Afterlap has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

When you choose **Online** to play with a friend, the game sends the following to our game server so your friend can see it:

- the name you type in (it doesn't have to be your real name);
- the room code;
- your lap count, close calls and whether you are still playing during a match;
- a random player ID that the game creates on your device. It isn't linked to your name, email, phone number or any other account.

This information is used only to run the match. It is visible only to the other player in your room. It is deleted automatically within a day of the room closing. We don't sell it, share it or use it for advertising.

The game server is run by Supabase (supabase.com) on our behalf.

## What stays on your device

The game saves your best score, stars, skins, awards, daily challenge progress, play statistics, sound setting, online name and random player ID on your own device so they are there next time you play. Deleting the app deletes them.

## Network

All game files, including fonts, are included in the app. Solo play makes no network requests. The game only connects to the internet when you open **Online**.

## Children

Afterlap is suitable for all ages. Online play shares only a chosen display name and game scores, and only with the one friend who has your room code.
