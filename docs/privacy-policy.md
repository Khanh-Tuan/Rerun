# Privacy Policy for Afterlap

_Last updated: [DATE]_

Afterlap is an arcade game made by [YOUR NAME OR STUDIO].

## The short version

You can play without an account. We never collect your real name, address, phone number, contacts or precise location, and there are no ads, analytics or tracking.

## What we collect

### When you play (with or without an account)

To keep the leaderboards fair, the game asks our game server to time each solo run. For each run the server stores:

- a random player ID that the game creates for your device (it isn't linked to your name, email or phone);
- when the run started and finished, the number of laps and close calls;
- the times at which you switched lanes during the run, used only to check that scores are genuine.

If you play without an account, this is not shown to anyone. Runs that aren't needed are deleted after a few days; your single best run is kept so you can claim it if you create an account later.

If you can't reach the server (for example, you're offline), you can still play; that run just won't count for the leaderboards.

### If you create an account (optional)

- **Your email address.** Used only to send you sign-in codes. It is never shown to other players and never used for marketing.
- **Your display name and country.** Shown on the leaderboards with your flag, best score and rank. You choose the name. The game suggests a country from your internet connection or your device's time zone; you confirm or change it. You can hide your country: then you only appear on the Global leaderboard, without a flag. We don't store your IP address.
- **Your online save.** A copy of your game progress (best scores, stars, skins, awards, daily challenges and play statistics) so you can continue on another device.
- **Whether you're playing right now.** Other players see a small "online" dot next to your name while you're playing.

### When you play online with a friend

The game sends your chosen name, room code, lap count, close calls and whether you're still playing, so your friend can see them. This is visible only to the other player in your room and is deleted within a day of the room closing.

### If you buy a premium skin (optional)

Premium skins are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card, Apple ID or payment details. The game only learns which skins your Apple ID owns, so it can show them as unlocked. If you have an account, the skins you own are part of your online save.

## How it's used

Only to run the game: timing runs, ranking scores, saving your progress and running online matches. We don't sell or share it, and we don't use it for advertising.

The game server is run by Supabase (supabase.com) on our behalf. If you have an account, sign-in emails are sent through our email provider on our behalf.

## What stays on your device

Your progress is also saved on your own device so it's there next time you play. Deleting the app deletes it from the device.

## Deleting your data

In the game: **Ranks** > your name > **Delete account**. This permanently deletes your account, display name, leaderboard entries, runs and online save from our server. Progress on your device stays until you delete the app.

## Children

Afterlap is suitable for all ages. Accounts are optional. Public information is limited to a chosen display name, an optional country and game scores.
